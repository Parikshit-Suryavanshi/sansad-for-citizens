Committee Report on OBC Representation and Welfare in Three Fertilizer Public Sector Undertakings

Report date: 2023-07-28

Committee: Committee on Welfare of Other Backward Classes

Lok Sabha: 17

Type: Adhoc Committees

What was discussed

The Committee on Welfare of Other Backward Classes examined the measures undertaken by the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers to secure representation of Other Backward Classes (OBCs) in employment and for their welfare in Madras Fertilizers Limited (MFL), Brahmaputra Valley Fertilizers Corporation Limited (BVFCL), and Fertilizers & Chemicals Travancore Limited (FACT).

The Committee took evidence from representatives of the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers, MFL, BVFCL, and FACT on May 20, 2022, regarding the subject. The report was considered and adopted by the Committee at its sitting held on July 25, 2023. It was presented to the Lok Sabha and laid in the Rajya Sabha on July 28, 2023.

What the report found

Madras Fertilizers Limited (MFL):

  • One official belonging to the OBC category is in the Management Committee.
  • As on December 31, 2021, MFL had a total staff strength of 514, with 253 (49.22%) employees belonging to the OBC category. 20 Backward Classes employees who joined prior to September 8, 1993, and were subsequently declared as OBC by Government of India notification, are currently on roll.
  • The company is in revival mode, and the overall strength of employees is less than the approved strength, as MFL has not undertaken recruitment in the last five years.
  • Representation of OBCs in supervisory and non-supervisory roles is above 27%.
  • An Internal Grievance Committee-OBC is functioning to redress grievances of OBC employees on service matters. Four complaints were received during the last two years regarding promotion, recovery of disciplinary amount, "on-duty" accident leave, and work from home for a nursing mother, which were closed.
  • Mr R K Balamurugan is the Liaison Officer for OBCs from March 9, 2020, to date. Except for Mr S Krishna Nagesh (from April 26, 2018 – January 30, 2019), all other Liaison Officers for OBCs during the last five years belonged to the OBC category.
  • Management holds periodic meetings with the OBC Employees’ Welfare Association. The General Manager – P&A meets regularly, and the CMD (Chairman and Managing Director) holds quarterly meetings; dates mentioned were December 17, 2020, April 1, 2021, July 7, 2021, and November 23, 2021.
  • CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility) funds are allocated annually based on Government Annual Theme. Activities for 2019-20, 2020-21, and 2021-22 (up to December 31, 2021) included supplying medical equipment, potable water, honorariums, distributing rice and groceries, and installing oxygen plants, with specified amounts spent.

Brahmaputra Valley Fertilizers Corporation Limited (BVFCL):

  • None of the officials in the Board/Top Management Body belong to the OBC category.
  • As on December 31, 2021, BVFCL had a total staff strength of 435, with 124 (28.50%) employees belonging to the OBC category.
  • In 1993, BVFCL was a unit of erstwhile Hindustan Fertilizer Corporation Ltd., and actual data for Group-A posts (operated at corporate level) is not available for September 8, 1993.
  • Reservation policy for OBCs is followed for direct recruitment, with 27% reservation for Group A posts on All India Basis by Open Competition, 25.84% for Group A posts for Direct Recruitment on All India Basis Otherwise than by Open Competition, and 27% for Group C and D posts.
  • Post-based Roster Registers are maintained as per Department of Personnel & Training (DoPT) norms. Year-wise vacancies, shortfalls, and backlogs for OBCs are indicated and carried forward.
  • The Registers are inspected/authenticated periodically by the Liaison Officer for OBCs, with no discrepancies highlighted.
  • One backlog vacancy from 2020 was advertised due to non-availability of suitable candidates, and no special recruitment drives were needed.
  • Age relaxation is extended to OBC candidates in recruitment.
  • No separate Backward Classes Cell has been set up. At present, the Manager (HR) (level E-5) is the Liaison Officer for OBCs. The Liaison Officers during the last five years (Shri Brinchi Kumar Sonowal, Sr. Personnel Officer, and Shri Dasmat Hembram, Manager (HR)) did not belong to the OBC category, but an officer belonging to the OBC category has recently been appointed as Liaison Officer for OBCs.
  • Grievances are redressed by organizing meetings with the Grievance Redressal Committee. No complaints were received from OBC employees during the last two years.
  • No OBC Employees’ Welfare Associations/Unions exist in BVFCL.
  • No specific welfare scheme under Corporate Social Responsibility for the OBC category exists in BVFCL.

Fertilizers & Chemicals Travancore Limited (FACT):

  • None of the officials in the Board/Top Management Body belong to the OBC category.
  • As on January 1, 2022, FACT had a total staff strength of 1706, with 681 (39.92%) employees belonging to the OBC category.
  • Roster registers are maintained for implementation of reservation for OBCs as per DoPT norms, with separate registers for each post/category for direct recruitment at the corporate level, and State-wise for Group C and D posts where local recruitment is used.
  • Year-wise vacancies for OBCs are indicated, and shortfalls and backlogs are carried forward.
  • Recruitment announced for some positions in 2019, 2020, and 2021 was kept on hold under instructions from the Department of Fertilizers (DoF), meaning not all advertised vacancies were filled.
  • Relaxation in age limit (3 years) and standards is provided to OBC candidates.
  • Shri. Deepak T P, Deputy Manager(HR)IR, is the Liaison Officer for OBCs. Liaison Officers are always in place and belong to the OBC category.
  • Complaints are considered by Liaison officers/HR officials, and Grievance Committees look into complaints of all employees. No complaints were received from OBC employees during the last two years.
  • No periodical meetings were held with OBC Employees’ Welfare Associations/Unions as no problems/grievances have been raised.
  • CSR funds were utilized for school education (Rs. 2.4 Lakhs in 2019-20), Covid-19 assistance and PM CARES Fund (Rs. 12.12 Lakhs in 2020-21), and Oxygen plants, tablet computers, insecticidal nets, and Covid assistance (Rs. 103.23 Lakhs in 2021-22 up to November 2021).
  • CSR activities have not specifically targeted exclusive benefits for Backward Classes, but due to the nature of activities, those belonging to Backward Classes also benefit.

What was recommended

  • The Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers should adhere to the Department of Public Enterprises (DPE) Office Memorandum (OM) No. 2(15)/2011-GM dated April 18, 2011, which states that if persons from the SC, ST, and OBC categories and Women candidates come forward with the criteria for appointment of non-official Directors on the Board of Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs), they should be given preference in appointment. (Applies to BVFCL and FACT)
  • Madras Fertilizers Limited (MFL) should maintain cadre-wise/post-wise data clearly showing OBC candidates selected as per reservation policy in direct recruitment to assess the implementation of reservation policy.
  • MFL should separately maintain data showing overall representation of OBC employees in the total strength, including those promoted as part of their career progression or in temporary employment. All such information should be uploaded on the organization's website.
  • Brahmaputra Valley Fertilizers Corporation Limited (BVFCL) should maintain cadre-wise/post-wise data clearly showing OBC candidates selected as per reservation policy in direct recruitment to assess the implementation of reservation policy.
  • BVFCL should separately maintain data showing overall representation of OBC employees in the total strength, including those promoted as part of their career progression or in temporary employment. All such information should be uploaded on the organization's website.
  • Fertilizers & Chemicals Travancore Limited (FACT) should maintain cadre-wise/post-wise data clearly indicating OBC candidates selected as per reservation policy in direct recruitment to assess the implementation of reservation policy.
  • FACT should separately maintain data showing overall representation of OBC employees in the total strength, including those promoted as part of their career progression or in temporary employment. All such information should be uploaded on the organization's website.
  • BVFCL should ensure that at least one OBC representative is appointed in their Grievance Redressal Committee to safeguard the interests of OBC employees.
  • FACT should ensure that at least one OBC representative is appointed in their Grievance Redressal Committee to safeguard the interests of OBC employees.
  • Details of Liaison Officers, including their name, e-mail IDs, and Telephone Nos., should be uploaded on the websites of MFL, BVFCL, and FACT.
  • Roster Registers, duly authenticated by Liaison Officers, should also be uploaded on the website of the respective organization.
  • The Committee hopes that BVFCL management will hold periodic meetings with the OBC Employees Welfare Association once it is formed, to address the grievances of the OBC employees.
  • FACT management should hold periodic meetings with the OBC Employees Welfare Association to redress the grievances of OBC employees.
  • MFL should identify backward regions with OBC domination for effective allocation and utilization of funds under Corporate Social Responsibility.
  • FACT should identify backward regions with OBC domination for effective allocation and utilization of funds in nearby Udyog Mandal areas under Corporate Social Responsibility.

What it means for citizens

This report focuses on ensuring fair representation and welfare measures for Other Backward Classes (OBCs) in three public sector fertilizer companies: Madras Fertilizers Limited (MFL), Brahmaputra Valley Fertilizers Corporation Limited (BVFCL), and Fertilizers & Chemicals Travancore Limited (FACT).

For OBC citizens seeking employment or who are already employed in these companies, the implementation of these recommendations could lead to more transparent reservation policies, improved grievance redressal mechanisms with OBC representation, and greater access to information regarding recruitments and OBC employee data. Additionally, the recommendations concerning Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) funds suggest that these companies should focus some welfare activities on backward regions with a high OBC population, which could potentially benefit OBC citizens in those areas.
