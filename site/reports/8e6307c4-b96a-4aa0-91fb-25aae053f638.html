Government Action on Concessions for Strategic Railway Gauge Conversion Works

Report date: 2008-04-17

Committee: Railway Convention Committee

Lok Sabha: 14

What was discussed

This Seventh Report by the Railway Convention Committee (2004) addresses the action taken by the Government on observations and recommendations contained in the Committee's Third Report. The Third Report, which was presented to the Lok Sabha and laid on the Table of the Rajya Sabha on 12th August, 2005, focused on extending concessions available for the construction of new railway lines on strategic consideration to gauge conversion works undertaken for strategic consideration.

The Third Report contained two observations/recommendations. The Ministry of Railways (Railway Board) provided action taken replies for both. The Railway Convention Committee considered and adopted this Draft Report at its sitting held on 04th January, 2008.

What the report found

The Committee noted that the Ministry of Railways (Railway Board) accepted its recommendation regarding the extension of concessions in dividend payment (a payment made by Railways, usually to the central government) to strategic gauge conversion works. These concessions were to be given effect from the financial year 2005-06. The Ministry of Railways also assured that due priority would be given for the completion of projects taken up on strategic consideration, subject to the availability of resources.

The Committee expressed satisfaction that concessions in dividend payment were applied to strategic gauge conversion works from 2005-06, and that the Ministry of Railways agreed to prioritize these projects. The Committee also requested to be informed about the total number of strategic projects under execution, the number of strategic projects given priority during the last two years, and the details of those projects.

What was recommended

  • Concessions in payment of dividend, as available for new lines constructed on strategic consideration, should also be extended to ongoing and future gauge conversion works taken up on strategic consideration.
  • Fresh vetting of these strategic projects by the Ministry of Defence is not required, as these projects are initiated based on the request or recommendation of the Ministry of Defence.
  • Due priority should be given for the completion of projects taken up on strategic consideration, because the issue of national security is of paramount importance.

What it means for citizens

The report indicates that railway gauge conversion projects identified as strategically important will receive financial benefits, specifically concessions in dividend payment (a payment made by Railways, usually to the central government), effective from the financial year 2005-06. Furthermore, the government has agreed to prioritize the completion of these strategic railway projects, as long as resources are available. This suggests a focus on developing and upgrading specific railway lines deemed crucial for national security.
